# Roadmap: what the site does not cover yet

Each item needs its own source-backed dataset before it can be published. Contributions are
welcome; see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

- **FY2026 audited actuals.** FY2026 is budget-only until the county publishes its FY2026 ACFR.
- **Audited actuals beyond the General Fund.** Budget-versus-actual covers the General Fund,
  FY2011 to FY2025. There is no all-fund audited spending series yet.
- **Transit.** Transit recipient amounts are printed in the FY2026 book but are not yet
  structured data.
- **Staffing and compensation.** Authorized positions, FTEs, salary ranges and compensation
  histories by department.
- **Department expense classes.** Personnel, operating and capital detail within departments.
- **Roads across funds and governments.** The site explains the separate published road lines;
  a reconciled total needs its own dataset.
- **Debt detail.** Debt service by issue.
- **Per-resident and inflation-adjusted comparisons.** These need population and price-index
  series with sources.
- **Property-tax history and bill line items.** The estimate uses 2025 millage. Earlier tax
  years, actual bill line items, fees and credits are not imported.
- **Search filters.** Filtering search by fund or domain.
